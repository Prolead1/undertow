\small
\textbf{Original contribution.} This project has the following original
contributions:
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0.15em}
  \item An exact, parity-checked testbed for concentrated-liquidity research:
  an integer fee-growth replay engine, a fast training simulator, and a harness
  that bounds their fee drift to about $10^{-7}$ USDC per step on recorded
  data.
  \item A joint risk-sensitive reinforcement-learning formulation in which one
  policy chooses the band width and the hedge ratio, with CVaR used both as a
  training penalty and as the checkpoint-selection statistic across five seeds.
  \item The first measurement at the level of a policy, to our knowledge, of
  learned hedge ratios in concentrated-liquidity provision, replayed against recorded
  on-chain events with gas, fees and slippage applied to both the liquidity
  and the hedge leg.
  \item An empirical active/passive boundary across two fee tiers, with an
  analytical loss-versus-rebalancing overlay, showing where the fee tier flips
  the passive sign and why recentring is dominated on the primary pool.
  \item A diagnostic account of why a standard agent fails on this task, which
  interface repairs restore learning, and an ablation showing the regime input
  buys tail protection rather than return.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
