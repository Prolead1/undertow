\section{Complete Per-Window Results}
\label{app:results}

The main body reports means over the evaluation windows. This appendix reports
every window for every rule so a reader can see the dispersion behind each
mean. Each table gives the window number, its dominant regime, PnL in USDC,
the annualised Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown as a percentage of capital, CVaR
at the $5\,\%$ level in USDC per tape event, and the liquidity leg's net excess
over the opening basket. The windows are the same for every rule; the final
window is the five-day partial period, which is why its magnitudes are smaller.

The tables cover the five benchmark rules, the five learned seeds of the main
run, and the two scripted recentring rules that complete the pre-registered
ladder. The learned seeds are evaluated at the checkpoint selected by
validation Sharpe on the training lane, never on these windows.
