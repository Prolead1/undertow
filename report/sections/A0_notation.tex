\section{Notation and Glossary}
\label{app:notation}

Table~\ref{tab:notation} collects the symbols used in the derivations and the
results. All monetary quantities are in USDC, the pool's token0, unless a
caption says otherwise; prices are quoted in USDC per WETH, so a rise in
$P_t$ is an appreciation of the risky asset.

The report uses the following abbreviations. An automated market maker (AMM)
quotes prices from a deterministic rule instead of an order book; Uniswap~v3
is the concentrated-liquidity AMM studied here\cite{adams2021uniswap}. A
liquidity provider (LP) supplies the pool. Loss-versus-rebalancing (LVR) is
the arbitrage loss the LP bears when the pool price is stale relative to the
wider market\cite{milionis2022lvr}; impermanent loss (IL) is the same gap
measured against a passive holding. A Markov decision process (MDP) is the
sequential-decision model. Proximal policy optimisation (PPO) is the training
algorithm\cite{schulman2017ppo} and generalised advantage estimation (GAE)
supplies its advantage estimates\cite{schulman2015gae}. Conditional
value-at-risk (CVaR) at level $\alpha$ is the mean of the worst
$1-\alpha$ fraction of outcomes; value-at-risk (VaR) is the corresponding
quantile\cite{rockafellar2000cvar}. Total value locked (TVL) is the value of
assets in a pool, maximal extractable value (MEV) is the profit available to
block builders from ordering transactions, and a perpetual swap (perp) is the
derivative used for the hedge.
