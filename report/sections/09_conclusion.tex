\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Concentrated liquidity promises more fee income while the price stays inside a
chosen band, and the same position that earns those fees is the position that
loses to informed order flow. An LP who wants to do better than accept that
must decide when to recenter, how wide to stay and how much risk to hedge, and
each decision is expensive and uncertain.

This report measures whether a risk-sensitive learned policy can make those
decisions jointly better than the best rules written by hand, and where active
management stops paying. Answering that required a world in which costs are
measured rather than assumed, so we built a testbed from three years of
recorded trades, with an exact replay engine for ground truth, a fast
simulator for training, and a parity harness between them. The study then asked
four questions: where fees fail, why a standard agent fails, whether a learned
policy wins out of sample, and where the boundary between active and passive
provision lies.

The measured verdict is narrower than the bargain promised. On the
$0.05\,\%$ pool in 2024 the liquidity leg of every rule ran a deficit
against the pure buy-and-hold basket, and no strategy in the ladder beat
that basket on PnL; active rebalancing fared worst. The learned policy
reproduced the width its seeds preferred without learning to time, failed
the pre-registered Sharpe test against the frozen static width (difference
$-0.733$, interval $[-1.355,-0.041]$), and passed the tail test against every
unhedged rule. Its best seed tied the static width on Sharpe and led it on
CVaR, and the delta-matched hedge bought a flat tail at a carry cost. On this
market, learning did not make active management pay; it produced a policy
whose value is defence, and it priced the insurance.

The boundary of the study is also a map for future work: the action space can
be extended with continuous width and an explicit exit decision, the boundary
sweep can cover more pairs and fee tiers, and latency, private order flow and
the exact funding of a real venue can be modelled rather than proxied. Each
extension is a re-run of the same testbed rather than a redesign of it, which
is the point of building the testbed before asking the question.
