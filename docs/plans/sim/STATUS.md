## ADRs raised

- **ADR-004** — RL library selection (`stable-baselines3 >= 2.9`), in `docs/decisions/004-rl-library.md`
- **ADR-007** — `undertow.data` sim-API loader module attribution (`TICK_BASE` imported from `.config`; `load_*` adapters in `.loader`), in `docs/decisions/007-data-api-loader-module-attribution.md`
- **ADR-008** — data ⇄ sim public-API surface reconciliation (superset of T16's baseline; drops phantom `tick_to_sqrt_price`; restores the public-API guard), in `docs/decisions/008-data-sim-public-api-reconciliation.md`
- **ADR-009** — sim price orientation: raw sqrt price, human amounts/price (USDC numeraire); V2 IL r=1.2 typo corrected to −0.004141, in `docs/decisions/009-sim-price-orientation.md`
- **ADR-010** — MRSJD step size: `dt` is the 10-minute cadence (`1/(6·24·365.25)`), correcting the literal `1/(6·365.25)` (4h) in `CONTRACTS.md` §7; σ = 0 allowed as pure-drift limit, in `docs/decisions/010-sim-mrsjd-dt-cadence.md`
- **ADR-011** — baseline policies encode ranges through the factored `Action`; full range is the widest snapped band (not byte-exact `MIN_TICK`/`MAX_TICK`), in `docs/decisions/011-sim-baseline-action-encoding.md`
- **ADR-012** — fee tier units: `fee_tier_bps` holds Uniswap pips (3000 = 0.30%), pool-fee denominator `1_000_000`, slippage = notional × (pips/1e6 + impact bps/1e4), in `docs/decisions/012-fee-and-pip-units.md`
- **ADR-013** — backtester fee growth reaches the exact integer engine through the accepted top-level `FeeGrowthReplay` facade (boundary-crossing and pre-existing `feeGrowthOutside` are exact; the unknown-pre-swap-price/window-lattice residuals are flagged on `BacktestLedger.fee_growth_exact`), in `docs/decisions/013-backtester-fee-growth-api.md`
- **ADR-014** — one reference series: additive `DataConfig.reference_symbol` (+ optional `[reference] symbol`) and a keyword-only `build_event_tape(reference_symbol=...)` restrict the multi-symbol reference and regime feeds to the configured primary before the backward joins; `MarketView` selects the same symbol at its door and raises on mismatch, in `docs/decisions/014-reference-symbol-selection.md`
- **ADR-015** — calibrated MRSJD anchored to the data module's regime labels (per-state drift/vol, Student-t tails, empirical transitions from consecutive labels); the unsupervised EM remains only as a fallback for label-less/sparse views, in `docs/decisions/015-mrsjd-regime-anchored-calibration.md`
- **ADR-019** — the simulator's `PoolEngine` becomes a float64 replica of the backtester's exact external-pool fee engine: marginal (synthetic, never-minted) agent positions, piecewise per-crossed-tick swap apportionment, external-lattice seeding from the ADR-018 boundary snapshot + tape mint/burn replay, price-derived crossing, and the protocol-fee split; the backtester additionally credits the first tape event's fee, in `docs/decisions/019-sim-exact-fee-engine.md`
- **ADR-020** — real-data liquidity replay in the float64 sim: `apply_liquidity_event` absorbs float64 rounding residues above `2**53` (relative tolerance, genuine over-burns still raise), and `LpEnvironment` memoises external-lattice warmup checkpoints every 10k events so a reset replays at most one stride instead of the whole lane, in `docs/decisions/020-sim-realdata-liquidity-replay.md`
- **ADR-021** — liquidity-replay tick crossings follow the recorded `slot0` tick interval `(min_tick, max_tick]`, not the price: a downward swap landing exactly on `boundary(T)` records `tick = T-1` while the price stays on the boundary, so the next upward move must still cross `T`. Fixes the real-lane active-liquidity divergence (first at block 15,369,829; `-3.65e19` vs `+1.19e19` by block 15,997,326) in `PoolEngine.step` and `FeeGrowthTracker.apply_swap`, in `docs/decisions/021-tick-based-crossing-selection.md`
- **ADR-022** — training throughput: `ObservationBuilder` precomputes sorted as-of indexes (reference/regime/gas) so each step binary-searches instead of re-scanning full feeds (11 ms → ~1.4 ms/step), and `TrainingConfig.vec_env = "subproc"` runs each `parallel_envs` slot in its own process via a picklable `EnvFactory` that reloads the view per worker (646 → 1833 steps/s with 4 workers), in `docs/decisions/022-training-throughput-observation-index-and-subproc-envs.md`
- **ADR-030** — thesis research framing: risk-sensitive RL for joint LP + hedge management; C1 limits / C2 failure modes / C3 learned joint policy / C4 active-passive boundary; S17–S23 program, in `docs/decisions/030-thesis-research-framing.md`
- **ADR-029** — RL fidelity: the §11 observation appends the live band's distances to both bounds and its width in tick-spacing units (18→21), and the reward's IL term is measured against the fixed initial HODL basket (never rebased at a rebalance) so the per-step reward is the change in `excess_vs_hodl`, in `docs/decisions/029-observation-geometry-and-excess-vs-hodl-reward.md`
